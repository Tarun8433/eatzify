Privacy Policy

Last updated 28 September 2026

This policy explains how [Registered business name] ("we") handles your personal data in Eatzify, in line with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

What we collect

  • Account: your mobile number.
  • Profile: age, sex, height, weight, activity level, goals and dietary preferences you enter, used to set your targets.
  • Logs: food, water, weight, measurements, workouts and notes you record.
  • Health apps: if you connect Apple Health or Health Connect, we read only the data you allow (such as steps). We never write to them.
  • Payments: order and subscription records. Card and bank details are handled by Cashfree Payments and never reach us.
  • Coaching: messages and check-ins, if you work with a coach.

Why we use it

To run the app: calculating your targets and meal plans, showing your progress, providing coaching you ask for, processing payments, and keeping the service secure. We do not sell your data or use your health information for advertising.

Who can see it

You. A coach, only if you choose to work with one, and only what the app shows them. Service providers that host our servers and process payments, under contract. Authorities, only where the law requires.

Your rights

From the app (You → Privacy) you can download a copy of your data or delete your account. Deleting your account erases your personal data, except records we must keep by law (such as payment records for tax purposes).

Security and storage

Data is sent over encrypted connections and stored on servers in India. Sensitive data on your phone is encrypted.

Children

Eatzify is for adults aged 18 and over.
