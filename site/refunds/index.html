Refunds & Cancellations

Last updated 28 September 2026

7-day refund

If you are not happy with a paid plan, you can ask for a full refund within 7 days of purchase. Write to [support email] with your registered mobile number and the order reference from your receipt.

After 7 days

Requests made more than 7 days after purchase are not refunded, including for the unused part of a longer plan. Your plan stays active until the end of the term you paid for.

Cancelling renewal

You can stop a plan from renewing at any time from the app (You → Subscription). Nothing further is charged, and the plan keeps working until its end date.

Upgrades

When you move from Basic to Pro, the unused value of your Basic plan is credited against the price of Pro and you pay only the difference. That credit is not refunded as cash.

How refunds are paid

Approved refunds go back to the original payment method through Cashfree Payments, usually within 5–7 working days of approval; your bank may take longer to show it.

Failed or duplicate payments

If you were charged but your plan did not activate, or you were charged twice for one order, contact us with the order reference. Any amount taken in error is refunded in full.
